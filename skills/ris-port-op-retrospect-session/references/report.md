# Bounded retrospective report

Read before the final response. Use the user's language. Return all seven
sections, explicitly empty where appropriate; do not manufacture findings.

1. **Summary.** Main evidenced improvements or explicit absence of justified
   findings. State partial/intermediate status prominently when coverage is partial.
2. **Scope and coverage.** Selected session/project if known, snapshot/cutoff,
   stopping status, proven participants and links, sources/versions, overviewed
   ranges/continuations and inaccessible/unseen/pruned regions. Distinguish late
   evidence from proven historical state. Do not claim complete overview from an index.
3. **Work map and metrics.** Compact request → decisions → tools/skills → handoffs
   → checks → result. Show available per-participant request peaks separately from
   sums/cache, units/semantics/provenance/quality/coverage, and interval unions,
   waits/unclassified where relevant. If measurements are absent say so; no
   invented zeros. Include compact episode/bypass dispositions, or place their
   full accounting in sections 4–6 with cross-references.
   For every observed script/SQL/bypass expose its need, then-available alternative,
   discoverability, observed cost, potential risk (or unknown), and disposition
   with causal basis. This accounting also applies to justified and gap-limited
   episodes; a finding-only list cannot account for all bypasses.
4. **Confirmed problems, prioritized.** Each finding includes the fields below.
   An empty section is valid. Keep unsupported suspicions out of this section.
5. **Hypotheses and missing checks.** Addressable episode, candidate mechanism,
   supporting/contrary evidence, alternatives and the specific check needed to
   distinguish them. Propose checks for a separate operation; do not execute them.
6. **Investigated justified signals.** Include why a suspicious script, threshold
   crossing, recovery or wait is justified; retain gap-limited bypass dispositions
   explicitly instead of silently omitting them or calling them justified.
7. **Analysis limitations.** Missing instructions, usage semantics, lost originals,
   unverified current state/outcomes, inaccessible ranges or resource limits and
   the conclusions each limits. Explicitly state no material gaps when evidenced.

## Finding fields

- Stable local ID, problem and safe addressable event/document evidence.
- Observed consequences **separate from potential risks**. No guessed cost/loss.
- Exactly one severity: high / medium / low, justified by observed consequences.
- Confidence(problem) and confidence(cause), each high / medium / low with its
  own basis; these are judgments, not statistical probabilities. A clear problem
  may have an uncertain cause. Keep cause hypotheses and alternatives explicit.
- Historical received instructions and available alternatives relevant to cause;
  current applicability: persists / eliminated / unknown, with revision/evidence.
- Concrete owner, episode-linked minimal change and counterfactual mechanism.
  For an eliminated issue acknowledge the current fix, not a duplicate change.
- Improvement check / observable comparison, and context, action and maintenance
  costs of the proposal (qualitative when unmeasured). Deletion is a valid change.

Before responding, reconcile final claims with actions and coverage. Check every
observed script/SQL/bypass has a disposition; every number has source, semantics
and quality; every recommendation has episode/cause/owner/change/check/cost;
problem and cause confidence are independently supported. Verify late/current
facts were not applied retroactively, absence of evidence was not failure, and
risks were not counted as losses. Remove secrets and unnecessary excerpts in
favor of safe references. The report is not full implementation review, feature
acceptance or independent approval. Return it and stop: no save, fix, task creation,
publication, completion or automatic next stage.
