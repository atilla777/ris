# Prepare one feature through verified decomposition

Own transitions, one author context/dialogue and the **combined result**. Component owners
retain their complete methods, scoped results and direct invocation; the adapter
owns tracker encoding/operations/publication. This project scenario needs no new
portable base. Stop after verified decomposition and separate tracker publication,
without executing or closing the feature/tasks.

## Scope, permissions and execution

Establish project/current copy, chosen feature or epic/roadmap context, actor,
known answers, linked prior work and permitted claim, copy/setup, document/decision,
Git and tracker effects. An epic is optional, never invented. Workflow permission
alone does not accept unseen content or authorize unrelated source/structure
changes; checked policy acceptance below is a separate basis.
For a full preparation request, these ordinary effects include registration/claim,
one safe copy, accepted-document saving, checks/independent review and repairs,
Git commit/integration/non-force push to the established target, same-ID record
reconciliation, decomposition and separate tracker publication. No new permission
is needed merely for an internal step or owner. Unrelated outgoing Git commits
are excluded; shared Dolt history needs the project's accepted policy or explicit
authority for its actual outgoing scope. Explicit user/project restrictions win.
If they require a separate publication decision, ask one specific question about
the missing authority; do not end the flow at local saving. Independent permitted
review/analysis may continue while publication is blocked.

Select this full route for an authorized request to prepare the chosen feature,
including continuation of an already established preparation dialogue. A request
only for requirements/design documents keeps the direct owners' scoped results;
selection alone or content acceptance alone does not start this flow. Resolve a
material ambiguity in the intended endpoint once, before dependent effects,
rather than silently selecting local-only or full preparation. “Accept both and
continue” in this flow preserves its endpoint, not a new local-save boundary.
Apply installed `ris-proj-lib-common` and its applicable dialogue/artifact/quality/
published-input resources; apply `ris-proj-op-resolve-context` with its owned
configuration resource for relevant sources, tasks adapter, needed shared paths
and `workflows.prepare_feature.auto_accept` with its checked provenance.
For shared paths consume its checked Git-derived source-folder/current-copy
association; never duplicate source-folder
discovery or invent a task directory. Resolve a new-assignment actor through the
selected adapter's claim owner and forward it unchanged; recovery requires
verified prior assignment/copy association, not a fresh session fallback.

Apply existing owners in this context or delegate bounded work when useful; no
executor, separate session or configured role is mandatory for each internal
action. For delegation only, prepare the consumed role (`feature-preparation`
or `task-decomposition`, including optional graph proposals) through context's role resource,
verify the configured agent is available and actually launched; no silent fallback.
Independent review **must** use the configured `task-review` role and actually run
in a separate context independent of all content authors/repairers. Prepare that
role through context and check availability/permissions and invocation evidence,
not a configured name or assumed model tier.
Unavailable required review blocks publication, not independent useful analysis.

Each acting context applies its selected owner's relevant method and required
resources before that operation; do not load every child method merely to route
work. In this context use bounded contributions, not repeated standalone entry
ceremonies: validate supplied project/copy/input/preflight results for the action
and do not restart discovery, dialogue, acceptance or publication already owned
here. Common's stable-result reuse rule applies; repeat affected context/preflight
only on material drift, copy change or loss. Live rights/ownership/status/blockers
and current publication conditions remain fresh before dependent writes.
For delegation use common's minimal artifacts handoff with exact accessible
candidate/basis and permitted contribution. The recipient reads missing complete
inputs without foreign-chat assumptions and returns material questions here,
never accepts for the human. Inspect checked effects/limits, not just “done”;
the lead checks the whole without copying owners' algorithms.

At entry, unless sufficiently checked here already, preflight the selected adapter's native task create/read/
update, claim, child/edge and separate publication capabilities as needed, with
local-write isolation, remote, entire outgoing-history authority and native limits.
Preflight `ris-proj-op-publish-task` for the intended authorized document subject,
target and full publication gates; the not-yet-prepared copy is a prerequisite
below, not publisher-owned setup. Missing capabilities/permissions block dependent
effects, not authorized read-only proposals. Claim/sandbox/read-back are not CAS,
distributed exclusion or strict uniqueness. Do not require a manual window or
transaction over graph and external evidence; return unsupported required guarantees.

## Register the selected feature and prepare its one copy

Accept three inputs: an existing Feature ID (including a preliminary roadmap
feature), a confirmed Epic ID plus a chosen new idea, or a chosen standalone
idea. The last needs neither an epic nor prior roadmap planning. Keep known IDs;
verify an epic's actual identity/scope before assigning a new feature to it.
For a chosen idea go directly to limited registration below, not mandatory epic
planning. Only an unselected outcome uses the selection branch that follows.

If the outcome is not yet selected, apply installed `ris-proj-op-show-plan`'s owned
`references/feature-candidates.md` as a bounded read-only selection contribution
for the needed preparation action. Honor existing preliminary feature IDs and
choose among those records, not invented product outcomes. Return parallel sets
to the caller; this one-feature flow chooses one without imposing global serial
work. All-blocked yields diagnosis, not extra features. Apply `ris-proj-op-refine-epic`
only for a real goal/boundary ambiguity, with local-only contribution here.
An actually empty epic, or evidenced coverage gap, permits applying installed
`ris-proj-op-plan-epic-features` under this authorized flow, after its concrete
human graph agreement and with explicitly local-only writes/publication debt here.
Reread verified existing records and obtain the feature choice before continuing.
No status/next request authorizes that planning write. Do not register abstract ideas.

Once chosen, apply `ris-proj-op-specify-feature` in **explicitly limited early
registration** mode: approved title/brief goal, stable project-scoped retry key,
known exact ID and optional confirmed epic, with tracker-write authority. Consume
the adapter's all-status collision/reuse/read-back result. No spec/design,
acceptance or document copy is required for that contribution. Require relevant
all-status dedup across parents too, including possible matches outside the named
epic. Reuse a matching ID; ambiguous/conflicting matches are not absence. Do not
automatically reopen closed records, move matches to the requested epic, or
overwrite started/foreign work. Resolve the actual conflict before dependent writes.
Require one confirmed task ID with approved goal and available scope/criteria, never a
replacement “ready” record. Preserve legacy payloads; missing/old/malformed JSON
alone never blocks the new document route or proves ordinary kind.

Apply installed specify-feature's `references/stage-prerequisites.md` to the
requested preparation actions once; reuse confirmed accepted prerequisites.
Refresh affected applicability on input/edge/scope change and live action blockers
before dependent writes. Readable prerequisites differ from implementation edges;
wait for needed decisions, not whole-feature closure or a replacement record.

Before the first document/evidence write, including drafts, require a checked
copy result from `ris-proj-op-prepare-task-worktree` for this ID/actor with purpose
`feature-preparation`. Pass authorized claim/copy/setup scope, repository/current
and context-checked primary roots, known association, path/branch and explicit
base or established remote/main rule. That owner composes the portable preparer
and selected worktree adapter; do not duplicate creation/preservation algorithms.
Reuse that result across writers, not another preparer call per write. Require
open own preparation assignment, fresh action-specific directed-blocker
checks and a safe own copy. Execution-only blockers do not automatically forbid
clarification, but actual/unknown preparation blockers and native refusal are not
bypassed. Keep execution gates unchanged. Recheck ownership/status/blockers before
each mutation batch through the selected adapter.

This is the same record's native assignment with purpose `feature-preparation`,
not a separate execution claim, stage task or new metadata field. In Beads the
existing `assignee/in_progress` and guarded assignment are sufficient; the request
and calling contract convey purpose. Preparation ownership grants no permission
to implement. Later execution rechecks its own inputs, authority and all native
execution gates, preserving the associated copy and foreign-work protections.

Consume the preparer's checked work/copy association and current-copy result,
separate copy/environment readiness, setup effects and limits; do not reconstruct
its infrastructure observations as another handoff checklist.
Setup not requested is not environment readiness. Use that same
prepared copy as the document root; refresh its local context/association once
after the copy change. Writers, review and publication consume that result unless
relevant settings/association drift or evidence is lost. Never write
on shared main first or ask the publisher to branch later. Preserve user/foreign
changes; no implicit reset/stash/move or dirty-primary transfer.

Apply common's published-inputs convention and check destination/identity before
document writes. New features use `sources.specs/<full feature ID>/spec.md` with
readable H1, Feature-ID/Summary/optional Keywords. No suffix or tracker pin;
title changes never move the directory/copy. Existing explicit legacy links/hints
lead to actual documents/decisions without migration or relocation. Write in this
same copy; no draft store, registry or second publication copy.
On pause/setup failure retain this ID/path/branch and existing documents; the copy
owner reconciles actual refs/registration/files/setup before only missing actions.
No mandatory transition journal, intermediate commit or draft file is introduced.

## One requirements and design dialogue

### Choose sufficient depth within the dialogue

Depth is a request argument: `auto` (default, also for old calls without a mode),
`compact` or `full`; an explicit human choice wins. Clarify unknown or contradictory
mode requests, never silently replace them. This adds no config key, tracker
field/state, role, classifier, assessment stage or intermediate artifact.

During ordinary input reading/dialogue, Auto chooses Compact when the outcome is
sufficiently defined and reasonably executable as one work unit. Consider known
requirements, applicable product/rules/architecture, ready framework/library
mechanisms, independent outcomes, scale/architectural risk and executor coordination.
Choose Full for concrete additional design, splitting or coordination needs, not
merely a new dependency, file count or changed product logic. Briefly explain the
reason; it is not a readiness gate. Explicit Full is never downgraded.

Explicit Compact cannot hide material uncertainty/risk: name the exact gap and
consequences, agree only the necessary expansion before dependent work, and retain
the usable ID/copy/answers/documents/decisions. Do not silently switch to Full or
restart preparation; a small clarification alone does not require Full.

Compact reuses applicable product/rules, accepted decisions, current code and
verified framework/library capabilities; ask only material gaps and do not redesign
ready mechanisms. Prefer the sufficient `spec.md` below with a separately selectable
inline technical approach and the feature itself as the default executable unit.
Apply the decomposition owner's one-task-first rule, not a ban on justified children
or a hidden depth switch. Full uses the same owners with deeper dialogue/design and
coordination only as needed; it mandates neither a long questionnaire, design.md/ADR
nor children. Both preserve all acceptance, independent review, copy/ownership,
checks, Git/same-ID/composition/Dolt and recovery gates and supply the existing
execute-task inputs unchanged. Compact prepares product features, not the limited
Fast execution exception; neither branch implements or closes work.

Depth `auto` is **not** boolean `workflows.prepare_feature.auto_accept`. Acceptance
mode below is determined independently for both depths and each content scope.
References to the full/whole preparation flow mean this complete route in either
depth, not only depth `full`.

Determine effective acceptance **separately for requirements and design** from
context's validated boolean: `true` (including omitted leaf/parents or absent
optional root) enables policy acceptance; `false` retains the manual protocol
below. Invalid/unreadable selected config blocks this branch, never defaults.
An explicit request to approve requirements, design or both makes only the named
scopes manual, without modifying config. Preserve attributable overrides and
reassess them when the person changes the request; merely answering substantive
interview questions is not an override or formal content approval.

Apply common `published-inputs.md`'s **Policy acceptance inside feature preparation**
for automatic scopes. Interview still resolves material choices, conflicts and
missing answers; never treat silence, an unresolved alternative or a policy flag
as that answer. Once inputs are sufficient, formulate/check complete candidates,
establish their exact policy basis and save through the existing owners without
requesting formal requirements/design confirmation or an intermediate reply.
Keep full text in the authorized documents, not a chat dump; communicate a short
result and links. Manual scopes alone use complete-candidate presentation and
human approval where their basis needs it. An explicit request for confirmation
is honored even when the named scope could otherwise be wholly inherited; it
does not rewrite its original source provenance. `false` without such a request
keeps the ordinary inherited/direct protocol. Mixed auto/manual scopes retain
independent bases; sufficient inherited parts keep their original derivation.
Enabling auto never renews unchanged existing acceptance or replaces a sufficient
inherited basis with a fabricated policy event. Use policy for the candidate
acceptance actually performed here, preserving every retained original basis.

Common's `published-inputs.md` owns the independently sufficient requirements/
design basis. Reuse its confirmed sources/decisions/derivation/applicability across
saves, publication and decomposition; reassess only affected changes or missing
evidence. Preserve original provenance. Policy can accept a resolved candidate,
not choose unresolved material behavior for the person.

Apply `ris-proj-op-interview-feature`'s **Map the material requirements** and
**Check and hand off** to known answers, relevant product/rules and actual sources.
This is coverage, not a mandatory interview or separate report. Use its conditional
interview route only for material gaps. Apply `ris-port-op-formulate-feature-spec`
to formulate/check the full requirements candidate, including observable `AC-nnn`,
and `ris-port-op-design-feature` to develop/check sufficient design against those
requirements, actual code/architecture and related contracts. Design may be proposed
against explicitly proposed requirements while exploring; it cannot be saved as
accepted until requirements and design each have that sufficient basis.
Resolve interdependent functional/technical choices in **this one dialogue**;
reuse known answers, investigate factual questions and ask only material gaps.
Prefer a compact requirements/design candidate from available sources and code,
then ask only remaining new material choices. A related dependency-ordered block
is allowed; a fork preventing a meaningful candidate is asked earlier. Neither
an interview nor a fixed question count is mandatory.
Use the existing focused technical interview method if genuinely needed, not a
second functional questionnaire. Return behavior changes to the requirements
candidate; do not silently accept them as design choices.

Preserve the design method's responsibility/state owners, public contracts and
dependency directions, errors/retries/concurrency, compatibility, affected active
decisions/AC and test seams. Alternatives/ADR are conditional on material choices,
not ceremony. Prefer a sufficient design as its own unambiguous section in
`spec.md`, with nonoverlapping requirements/design selectors. Use `design.md` when
separation is useful or appending to already accepted whole-file requirements
would change their scope. Absence of design.md is not a gap; insufficient design
or missing sufficient design basis is. Ordinary specs are not OKF bundles.
Keep the spec sufficient, not long: result/bounds/exclusions, observable AC with
practical how-to-check, independently selectable sufficient design and material
open questions. Do not maintain a task backlog, execution status or checklist in
it. There are no section/length quotas. Design fills material gaps and cites
applicable architecture/contracts instead of reciting them; use verified ready
framework/library mechanisms instead of redesigning them. Domain-specific
prompts and deferred parameters apply only when relevant.

For manual scopes, before deciding new material show the **complete, distinguishable versions/scopes**, accessible
inline or at checked exact addresses. Explain design essence/flow, reused/new
technologies and why, tradeoffs, material risks and hard-to-reverse choices in
plain language; a short complete design can itself suffice. A summary never
accepts unseen content. Optional full draft storage needs explicit path/write
authority and the design owner's draft branch; it is not accepted save/publication.

One explicit human act may decide new material in **both** requirements and design
when it identifies both exact complete content selectors/versions and the new
material accepted, with authorized acceptor provenance. Keep two independently
identifiable sufficiently grounded results even if a decision address is shared.
Wholly inherited scopes skip new human approval unless explicitly requested.
Requirements acceptance alone
does not accept design. Automatic scopes needing a new acceptance instead get
their checked policy decision, never a fictional human act. Apply the specify/design owners' installed
`references/acceptance-and-save.md` for their respective scope; each verifies its
own complete candidate and sufficient basis, without demanding a second answer
merely because it is called separately. No new acceptance schema/file is required.

Apply `ris-proj-op-specify-feature` and `ris-proj-op-design-feature` as **explicitly
limited accepted-local-save contributions** for their document scopes, no record
refs or duplicate Git/tracker publication. For design use its document-only local
accepted-input branch with the verified local requirements basis;
the known ID remains the copy/review association, not a demand for already
published requirements. Pass the same checked copy, exact destinations, complete
sufficiently grounded text/version/selectors, original sources/derivation and
applicable actual decisions plus write authority. Identify these as internal
prepare contributions with each scope's effective mode, checked setting/default
provenance, attributable prepare-request authority and explicit limits/overrides.
A config value alone never changes a direct owner's acceptance contract.
Check basis/content read-back
and preserved sections. Missing/changed/ambiguous
inputs return here, never guessed acceptance or reformulation during save.
These local-only limits are internal contributions, not limits on the full
request. After checking both saves, the lead continues immediately to independent
review and the remaining authorized route below. Do not promote a child's `ok`
or preserved documents into whole-flow completion; missing authority requires
the concrete decision question, and technical failure the exact blocker.

On revision preserve published pins and original decision addresses. Briefly
identify material replacement and assess its basis under the scope's effective
policy/manual mode; obtain human answers for new material uncertainty. Policy
repairs require neither formal confirmation nor full-text chat duplication.
Requirements changes require design-owner applicability review; compatible
unchanged design keeps its acceptance. Uncertain applicability blocks dependent
work, not independent analysis; unchanged compatible content is not reaccepted
or repinned merely for save retries/integration.

## Independently review, publish Git and reconcile the same record

Draft review before final decisions is optional, used when it can usefully expose
material gaps. It is not a mandatory extra analysis round before publication
review. A wholly inherited candidate can go directly to publication-purpose
review. Reuse an already sufficient independent assessment only where its purpose,
full final snapshot, inputs and checks are applicable under the review owner.
After changes obtain necessary independent reassessment of the new full snapshot,
possibly from the same still-independent reviewer using unaffected evidence.
Human acceptance and review remain different facts; authors cannot self-approve.

Require actual independent `ris-proj-op-review-task` assessment of the **complete
document subject**: requirements/design and decisions against rules, AC and architecture,
exact full base+overlay/add/delete/modes snapshot and all authors/repairers.
Launch it when no already applicable independent assessment covers that subject.
Use publication-gate purpose and context-checked primary
`workspace.tasks/<confirmed-ID>/review.yaml` unless an exact authorized override
applies. Apply its installed `references/report-format.md`: one full local YAML,
explicit round, separate validity and external snapshot/input/check/provenance
applicability, no fallback past later adverse/unknown rounds. Review is not human
acceptance. Return high/medium findings to content owners, repair/check, obtain
targeted decisions only for changed material under its effective policy/manual
mode and fresh independent review. New material uncertainty returns to the human;
review approval never supplies content acceptance.
Use the machine verdict under that owner: applicable independent `approved`
proceeds, `changes_required` repairs/re-reviews, `blocked` or missing/invalid/
unsupported/inapplicable evidence stops dependent progression without a repair
loop or fallback to old approval. New YAML uses /2; existing /1 is conditional.

Apply `ris-proj-op-publish-task` for accepted documents from the **same prepared
ID-associated copy/branch**. Pass checked association/current state/base, exact paths,
accepted content/decision selectors, checks, independent review/provenance and
full prospective tree, target evidence and permissions. Consume its existing
whole-outgoing-history/integration/reassessment/non-force/remote-read-back result;
do not repeat its algorithm. Check applicable accepted project policy and pass
its actual source/version/acceptance and boundaries, or an explicit local request,
to the publisher; without either keep remote-only. Primary maintenance is reported separately: skipped
or failed local sync does not block same-ID reconciliation, decomposition or Dolt.
Any policy-required or explicitly requested local/dependency update remains an
unfinished part of the combined result, not
permission to replay a confirmed push or mutate a busy copy.

Consume the publisher's exact content/decision match for both accepted scopes,
without rereading unchanged basis or repeating publication checks. Resolve
decision-only `containing-commit` at the original immutable decision address.
After verified required Git publication apply specify's **limited record-only
reconciliation** to the same exact ID with expected prior state and approved
ordinary goal/scope/criteria. Check discoverability of published documents and
actual decisions, not a duplicate accepted-ref list. If only design remains use
design's limited reconciliation without duplicating requirements/design refs.
Inspect actual ID/type/optional parent and preserved content. No JSON/verification
update, replacement ID, renewed acceptance or second push. Actual identity/content
conflicts stop dependent writes; legacy JSON schema alone does not.

## Verify sufficient decomposition, then publish the tracker

Apply `ris-proj-op-decompose-feature` with the same ID, current published accepted
requirements and sufficient design, constraints/contracts/test seams, existing
task IDs and permitted child/edge effects. Explicitly request its **limited local
write/verification contribution**; this flow owns the final tracker publication.
Use its entire applicable portable/project method and coverage/reconciliation checks,
reusing the checked published inputs under common, in this session or a suitable executor without formal complexity
thresholds. Start with its sufficient one-task outcome in either depth; the full
multi-unit method is conditional on justified independent results or existing
children requiring reconciliation. No multi-task analysis is mandatory without
such grounds. Never skip coverage, actual complete all-status child/blocker reads
or native guards. Do not substitute a new split algorithm or Markdown backlog.

Do not ask the human to reapprove an obvious contract-preserving split. Return
material changes to scope, delivery or obligations for a targeted decision.
Check its complete result: for one unit, the existing feature itself has sufficient
scope/criteria/accepted coverage, reconciled complete child set and directed
blockers, without an artificial child. For multiple units, every child has own
result/scope/criteria, applicable accepted selectors/decisions, `<ID>#AC-nnn`
coverage, contracts/test seams/exclusions, stable key and only necessary directed
`blocks` edges (blocked → blocker), with exact ID/content/parent/edge read-back.
Known, sufficiently defined accepted future prerequisites may remain unfinished
in a complete decomposition when necessary directed blocks are verified; this
does not make dependent tasks executable. Unknown or disputed contracts block
only dependent decomposition, preserving independently sufficient results and
their actual publication state without claiming whole-flow success. Unmatched
children, missing AC/seams or cyclic edges remain gaps. Native refusal and actual
decomposition blockers are not bypassed. A one-task content gap returns to
authorized record reconciliation; it does not permit an implicit adapter update
or fake child.

After the owners' local checks invoke the adapter's **separate tracker publication**
for the verified ordinary record, one-task result or children/edges and any
authorized refinement/early-registration debt. Check authority for the entire
shared-main outgoing scope, ordinary non-force publication and fresh selected
remote revision/reachability evidence alongside local composition/coverage.
No-change verifies required availability/debt without empty push. Remote revision
assurance is not independent field equivalence. Do not close, claim execution,
plan technical steps or launch implementation after decomposition.

## Partial effects, resume and result

On ambiguous/failed writes stop dependent mutations and use the corresponding
owner to reconcile actual local/remote effects before retry. Keep the same ID,
copy/path/branch, published commits/decision pins and known child IDs/keys/edges.
Resume from current Git/Beads/accessible accepted inputs and applicable review,
using a new explicit depth request first, otherwise the accessible earlier request,
otherwise Auto on current facts. Reassessment cannot cancel accepted content,
shrink required composition or repeat confirmed effects. No saved mode marker is
needed to resume an old sufficient feature. Use evidence, not a state file or stale
success token. Recover each exact policy/manual basis,
historical setting/default and explicit overrides from saved decisions before
dependent work; today's config cannot manufacture prior acceptance.
Unknown create/setup/push is inspected
first; confirmed publication is not repeated for pending read-back, primary sync,
record update, decomposition or tracker debt. Revalidate only affected inputs/
checks/review; unchanged acceptance stays. No rollback of foreign work or blind
recreation is allowed. Missing evidence/owner blocks that step precisely.
Apply common's artifacts lost-review/check/acceptance recovery, without invented
historical pass or replay of confirmed push/close. Closed records stay closed;
this flow grants no closure or implementation authority. Unknown ownership/setup/
remaining process blocks dependent mutation. Infrastructure algorithms stay with
their owners; inspect returned results and applicability.

Reconstruct the actual preparation remainder: before Git, retain the candidate,
basis, checks/review and copy; after Git but before reconciliation, reuse that
publication and repair only the same record; after reconciliation or partial
children/edges, compare the full required composition with actual records before
adding only missing work; after verified composition, finish only tracker debt.
Unknown channels remain unknown until their owner verifies them. Ordinary final
notes may identify composition/coverage and remainder, but neither a child count
nor a `Preparation: complete` line proves completion or becomes required. Without
such notes resume/navigation can inspect existing documents, review, graph and
publication evidence; old sufficient features need no marker or migration.

Return one combined `ok|blocked|conflict|error` with exact feature/docs and separate
accepted scopes/decisions; confirmed Git, review address/round and separate tracker
publication; sufficient one-task or actual children/edges/coverage/prerequisites.
Include only needed project/copy provenance and effects/limits under common's
minimal handoff; local maintenance remains separately scoped. For partial effects
name confirmed channels and only missing recovery.
`ok` requires reviewed accepted published documents, matching same feature record,
verified sufficient decomposition and confirmed separate tracker publication;
component `ok`, ID/copy existence or closed prerequisites alone are insufficient.
Check that a new session can discover published inputs/decisions from project+ID
without chat, ignored notes or mandatory receipt forwarding. Stop at preparation,
not implementation or feature acceptance/completion.
