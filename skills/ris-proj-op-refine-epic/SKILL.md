# Clarify one epic's goal and boundary

Own only the evidenced epic clarification, not a feature graph. This project-only
result depends on the actual epic and sources; direct invocation works without a
role or separate agent. A clear unchanged outcome may return `changes: []`; size
alone is not an ambiguity. Do not maintain a second feature planning algorithm.

Apply installed `ris-proj-lib-common` and its conditional resources before governed
actions; apply installed `ris-proj-op-resolve-context` with its configuration/path
resources for relevant rules/product/specs and tasks adapter. Check returned scope,
status and provenance. Establish project, exact epic ID, accepted goal, the actual
ambiguity, prior commitments and authority. Full checked refinement/publication
is default; explicit proposal-only is read-only, explicit local-only returns the
publication remainder to its caller. Missing authority does not imply full success.

Through the selected installed compatible tasks adapter, read exact epic/type,
status, goal membership/content, directed blockers and relevant all-state related
epics/work needed to protect existing commitments. Read linked sources/decisions
where materially needed using common's published-input method. Freely structured
product material supplies desired behavior, not an automatic epic/feature list.
Missing/failed reads are unknown, not absence; no direct tracker commands here.

Clarify only the unclear goal/outcome, boundary, exclusions or completion intent
against accepted goals, relevant constraints and actual completed/started work.
Check linked source changes and light architecture/shared-contract impacts when
they can affect that clarification. Preserve accepted and foreign work. Return
material contradictions, cross-epic overlap or goal changes for explicit decision;
do not move scope, rewrite product or manufacture feature candidates. If already
clear, explain the evidence and no-op rather than inventing subdivisions.

Show the precise content delta and obtain human agreement before writing; generic
refine permission is not acceptance of unseen changes. Unchanged agreement is not
renewed. Only approved changed content fields of the confirmed open epic may be
updated through `tasks.epics.update`, with fresh expected-value comparison, targeted
native guards and independent read-back. Preserve status/assignee/membership/edges,
closed history and unrelated content. No feature registration, edge mutation,
claims, task splitting or next-operation launch. Preliminary feature registration
elsewhere does not require accepted specs/design/AC; this operation does not own it.

Preflight remote-gated writes/publication via the adapter owner. On failed,
ambiguous or conflicting write stop further writes, reread actual effects and
return preserved IDs/delta and exact remainder; no blind replay or rollback.
Fresh-read the epic and relevant scope/blockers, verify agreed changes and preserved
fields. Fresh comparison/read-back is not universal graph/content CAS. In full mode
apply the adapter's separate tracker-publication owner with actual destination and
outgoing-history authority; inspect its fresh revision/reachability receipt, not
just a push response. No-op checks required availability/debt without empty push.
No synthetic Git step or copied Dolt algorithm.

Return `ok|blocked|conflict|error`, project/config/epic/source provenance, ambiguity
and supported clarification, agreement, actual changed/preserved fields, local and
published facts separately, evidence/limits and exact unfinished work. A proposal
is unsaved, not accepted or persisted; local-only is not full publication. Stop
at clarification, without selecting work or planning the feature graph.
