# Formulate a product foundation

Own the **checked, read-only content proposal**, not acceptance or storage.
Work from direct inputs under the actual project's instructions, including
outside RIS. No RIS configuration, common/context skill, tracker, document
layout or OKF format is required. A caller owns project acceptance, formatting,
saving and the combined result. Do not write files or records, initialize a
project, choose a stack or architecture, plan a roadmap or individual features,
implement the product or configure build/test/format/CI. Do not start or select
another lifecycle stage.

## Establish inputs and investigate

Establish create/update, the product and requested boundary, available materials
and their versions/currency, existing decisions, constraints and prior answers
with sources/status. A direct request plus accessible relevant documents,
instructions and implementation evidence is sufficient input; no full product
design, repository or universal input schema is mandatory. Inspect these before
asking questions. Distinguish absent material from inaccessible material and
explain which conclusions unavailable sources limit.

Update additionally requires the existing foundation and an explicit change
boundary. Missing input blocks update, never silently becomes create. Reuse
applicable existing content, structure, identities and confirmed answers; inspect
adjacent content only as needed for consistency, not a whole-product rewrite.
Code can establish current behavior, not acceptance of future product intent.
Keep observed facts, accepted decisions, proposals, assumptions, disputed,
unknown and deferred matters distinguishable and attributed. Neither a file's
existence nor repetition of a proposal proves acceptance. Surface conflicting
sources rather than silently selecting one.

For material **interdependent** gaps, load and actually apply installed
`ris-port-op-interview` using the host skill loader or its actual installed
location. Supply the bounded product topic, decision areas and prerequisites,
known answers with sources/status and the intended foundation result. Cover
material branches within that boundary in one dialogue; do not copy its method,
start a second interview or repeat available confirmed answers. A single
clarification is asked directly; sufficient inputs need no interview. If the
dependency is unavailable, return useful independent content and the precise
blocked part, not an imitation interview. Incorporate its handoff and assess
subject sufficiency here; interview completion is not content acceptance.

## Formulate and check the substance

Prepare a coherent foundation in the person's/project's language, addressing:

1. **Purpose:** why the product exists and the intended useful outcome.
2. **Users:** who uses or benefits from it and their relevant context.
3. **Problems/tasks:** what those users need to accomplish or resolve.
4. **Main capabilities:** the broad means of meeting those needs, linked to
   users/tasks; distinguish currently observed behavior from intended capability.
5. **Boundaries:** included scope and meaningful exclusions, including adjacent
   responsibilities the product will not take on.
6. **Material constraints:** established business, operational, technical or
   other limits that actually affect this product; do not invent a stack.
7. **Unknown/deferred matters:** unresolved choices, source gaps and conflicts,
   with their impact on specific conclusions/operations and return triggers
   where known. An explicitly deferred architecture choice stays deferred.

These are substantive coverage areas, not seven mandatory files or empty
headings. State what evidence supports each material assertion or label it as
a proposal/assumption. Reuse a sufficient accepted foundation after checking
applicability rather than rewording it for ceremony. Do not demand a full future
design or prematurely decide technology to fill a section. A proposal may be
sufficient with explicit nonblocking unknowns; a gap in purpose/users/tasks or
another essential claim must not be hidden behind a generic template.

For update, return the precise old → proposed content delta, rationale and
affected scope while preserving unaffected content and accepted decisions.
Check the scoped result against sources and constraints, coverage of the areas
above, user → task → capability coherence, exclusions and neighboring claims.
Identify necessary adjacent decisions without performing their operations.
Unresolved contradictions block their dependent claims, not independent work.
Do not infer implementation readiness from documentary sufficiency.

## Return and stop

Return a distinguishable proposal version and scope with its complete substantive
text (or exact bounded amendment), source locators/versions and decision statuses,
reused decisions/answers, proposed changes and rationale, unknowns/deferred issues
and their impact, checks and unavailable evidence. Make it usable without access
to this chat; explicitly forward response-only material on a future handoff.

If essentials are missing, identify the partial usable content and exact missing
input/decision instead of calling it complete. State that this proposal was
neither accepted nor saved by this operation; any reused acceptance retains its
original scope and version. Reading/review of content is not live behavior or
product implementation verification. Stop after the checked proposal; do not
request final acceptance, save it even if the person agrees, or name a next skill.
