# Investigate a bug

Own an evidence-based report from direct inputs. Work without requiring project
configuration, a tracker, a document layout or another skill. Follow the actual
target project's rules and the supplied research permissions. A composing caller
owns its project integration, persistence and combined result. Investigation does
not authorize a fix, acceptance of new behavior, publication or message closure.

## Establish the bounded question

Use the supplied message/context, expectation or its sources, code/version,
environment, observations and allowed research scope. Inspect accessible sources
before asking only for material missing facts. There is no mandatory questionnaire
or requirement that the reporter know the cause. Preserve the original meaning;
reported behavior, independently observed behavior and assumptions are distinct.

Identify the expected result and its authority: an accepted requirement, current
contract or explicit authorized decision. Locate the alleged divergence and the
conditions in which it matters. Record exact source locators/versions and what
code/environment can actually be inspected. Do not silently substitute a convenient
expectation, version or environment. Conflicting/stale sources or ambiguous expected
behavior return the specific affected decision to its authorized owner; retain
independent findings and stop only dependent diagnosis/recommendations.

## Confirm and diagnose proportionately

Choose the cheapest sufficient confirmation appropriate to the result: an
applicable existing observation, a bounded reproduction/test for executable code,
or reading/static checking for documents, instructions or configuration. Apply
project prohibitions and required checks. Check reused evidence against the
inspected revision and conditions; a log from another version is not automatically
current. State commands/methods, observed results and limits; never claim an unrun
check or infer a fix from the reporter's message.

Trace the local path and affected boundaries until the evidence supports a bounded
recommendation and meaningful success/regression checks, or exposes a precise gap.
Neither a final root cause plus reproduction pair nor exhaustive system analysis
is mandatory. Unknown cause can coexist with a confirmed defect; a likely cause
does not confirm it. Label hypotheses and their discriminating evidence. An
unavailable environment or failed reproduction leaves a gap, not proof of correct
behavior. Return before materially expanding research/check cost or scope without
authorization, keeping useful evidence and a limited next step.

Check whether the recommendation restores an existing contract inside the allowed
boundary. A public-contract, data-model, responsibility or invariant change is a
material decision for its authorized owner before dependent work. Do not disguise
new desired behavior as a bugfix or automatically start full feature preparation.
An unexplained small diff is not evidence of locality.

If evidence proves correct behavior, explain why the reported conditions satisfy
the applicable expectation, including contrary observations. If evidence proves
a duplicate, identify the exact original and show the same defect/conditions;
title similarity is insufficient. Distinguish a new regression from an old closed
report. Identify useful information missing from the original for a caller to
preserve; do not mutate or close records yourself.

## Return and check the report

Provide a coherent report, with explicit unknowns rather than invented content:

- Subject and allowed scope; expected behavior, its basis and exact source versions.
- Manifestation/actual divergence and relevant conditions.
- Inspected code revision (including relevant dirty state) and environment;
  distinguish reported from actually checked versions/conditions.
- Confirmation method, evidence locators, results and applicability limits.
- Established facts separately from hypotheses; cause or precise remaining gap.
- Recommended bounded correction or disposition, constraints/accepted decisions,
  success criteria and affected regression checks; material unresolved decisions.
- **Defect confirmation:** confirmed, unconfirmed, proved correct, or proved
  duplicate, with its evidence. **Fix readiness:** sufficient or insufficient,
  with the exact reason, independently of confirmation.

Sufficient fix inputs establish required behavior/basis, a confirmed defect,
authorized bounded scope, meaningful fix/check criteria and sufficient technical
constraints or solution. Research sufficiency is not acceptance or execution
authority. For proved-correct/duplicate messages state that a fix is not proposed;
give the disposition proof and original ID/useful delta when applicable.

Compare conclusions to sources and contrary evidence. Each material assertion
must be traceable; separate a recommendation from an accepted decision. Return
findings even when persistence or further research is unavailable, naming missing
evidence/permission and the smallest useful next action. Stop at the report; do
not register, save into a tracker, implement, publish or launch another stage.
