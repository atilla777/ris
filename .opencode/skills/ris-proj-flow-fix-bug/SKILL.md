# Fix one registered bug

Own admission, necessary design, execution-input preservation, transitions and
the sufficiency of the combined fix result. This project-only scenario needs no
portable pair: diagnosis and implementation methods already have owners. Require
the selected project, **exact native bug ID**, actor and authorized fix scope.
Never choose another bug, convert its type, invent an epic/feature record or stage
children, or silently begin investigation. Registration, investigation and fixing
are separately requested results.

## Prepare and reconcile before effects

Ensure installed `ris-proj-lib-common` is available and apply it, with artifacts
before handoffs/writes, quality before assessment and dialogue before decisions.
Apply `ris-proj-op-resolve-context` and its owned configuration resource for rules,
selected tasks adapter, needed sources/shared paths and execution roles. Use its
checked explicit-or-verified-saved primary selection/provenance for shared paths;
do not derive a primary from Git. Read the actual project rules. Missing essential
inputs/dependencies/authority block their dependent work, never select a fallback.

Through the selected available adapter exact-read expected type `bug`, current
report, aligned acceptance criteria, decisions, original message, status, assignee,
complete children and directed blockers. Retrieve the full report from its clearly
delimited description section, or the exact published Git path/revision linked
from the bug. A file-backed report must be retrieved, not inferred from a summary;
local ignored notes/chat cannot be its only source. Ambiguous current reports,
misaligned criteria or stale/conflicting inputs require precise reconciliation.

Check advertised ordinary native bug reads, claim, execution snapshot append/read,
result persistence and separate fix-completion, plus tracker publication. For
Beads these belong to `ris-proj-adapter-beads-tasks` and its conditional
`references/bug-execution.md`; feature-record/1 and investigation disposition do
not supply them. Delegate bounded tracker operations to that owner with exact
content, identity, expected state and authority; inspect actual read-back.

Use the existing `task-execution` role for delegated noninteractive admission,
design formulation/save and snapshot preparation, with bounded owner/inputs,
allowed effects, accessible outputs and early-return predicates. Check configured
agent availability and actual launch; a configured name proves neither. Keep all
human decisions and separate acceptance in this **one leading dialogue**. Missing
decisions return here, never a nested interview. Do not create new roles or split
internal reads into roles. The execution flow keeps its own executors, independent
review and role gates; do not wrap it in another full execution agent/dialogue.

For prior or ambiguous effects first reconcile actual design, snapshot markers
and content, execution receipts, Git, closure and tracker publication. A closed
bug is never reopened or fixed again. Return its verified state, or hand proven
own unfinished execution to the execution flow's existing read-only recovery to
finish only remaining verification/publication. A worktree/assignee alone does
not establish that execution. Foreign claims, active blockers or unfinished
mandatory children stop dependent work with their exact boundary.

## Admit sufficient inputs and resolve only necessary design

Read the substance, reusing established facts without a questionnaire. Require:

- Required behavior and its accepted requirement/current contract/authorized
  decision, with source versions; research readiness is not requirements acceptance.
- Confirmed actual divergence with applicable evidence and inspected code/environment.
- Authorized bounded correction, meaningful success and affected-regression checks.
- Sufficient technical constraints or a justified local solution preserving contracts.

An unknown final cause alone is not a blocker when those inputs are sufficient.
If the only remaining gap is a necessary material technical choice, enter the
design branch below before execution. If other essential inputs are missing,
return the exact missing evidence, authority or decision and a bounded next result,
then stop. With sufficient inputs proceed to basis preservation and execution.
Do not diagnose anew, fabricate a cause, tune the expectation to the defect or
automatically prepare a feature.

A sufficient report replaces formal separate spec/design files. If a public
contract, data model, responsibility, invariant or other consequential choice
must change, use `ris-proj-op-design-feature` on the **same ordinary bug** with the
report/criteria and accepted constraints. The design owner applies its portable
method and version/save/review/publication gates. Obtain actual separate acceptance
of the exact design in the leading dialogue; forward its full versioned content
or accessible candidate and decision for bounded save. Name the agreed document
path without inventing a feature slug. A justified local correction inside existing
contracts needs no separate design unless project rules require it. Conflicting
expectations return to their authority before dependent edits. Reassess only affected
inputs/results; unchanged accepted decisions stay pinned.

## Preserve the basis, then use the whole execution route

After necessary decisions, freshly compare report, aligned criteria and constraints
with the admitted basis. Ask the adapter to append/reconcile **one full recoverable
historical execution-input snapshot** in bug notes under a stable project/bug-scoped
identifier. Include full report text, report identity/version/author/code revision
and source locator, exact fix/check criteria, accepted decisions/constraints and
separate design content/reference/acceptance when needed. A number/hash or link
without recoverable text is insufficient. Inspect marker AND complete content and
occurrences before and after; reuse exact existing effects, stop on conflicting or
ambiguous identity, reconcile lost responses before retry. Native append preserves
foreign notes but promises neither append-once nor report+append atomicity. The
snapshot is historical input, never a second current report or a replacement for
the investigation owner's protected report/acceptance persistence.

Pass the **same exact ID**, full snapshot text and locator (project/tracker + ID +
marker), report source/version, accepted decisions, permissions, copy/effect receipts
and limits to `ris-proj-flow-execute-task`. Apply that scenario **in full**, including
its conditional plan policy, copy/claim, roles, code TDD or text owner, independent
review, Git publication, separate exact completion and tracker publication. Do not
copy or skip its stages. In RIS its mandatory persisted local plan remains; direct
plan-task still saves a plan. No new slash command is required.

At handoff and resume compare current report/criteria/decisions with the historical
snapshot. Material changes go to affected plan/change/review/evidence owners for
targeted applicability assessment before dependent effects; compatible results may
be retained with reasons. Preserve old snapshots; a newly needed basis gets its
own identified full snapshot rather than rewriting history. Existing published or
closed effects remain facts, not permission to reopen or replay them. After transfer,
the execution flow owns recovery through its existing route.

## Verify the combined result and stop

Inspect component artifacts and receipts, not success assertions. Require criterion
coverage of the original divergence and affected regressions on the exact candidate/
published revision, applicable project checks and independent review. Never claim
unrun checks or live reliability from text/static inspection. Return promptly to
the leading dialogue on accepted-contract/platform conflict, unavailable essential
guarantee/input or materially expanding verification before dependent experiments.

Return `ok|blocked|conflict|error`, exact ID/type/project/config/actor/copy, report
and snapshot locators/versions, sufficient basis and separate decisions, actual
changes, checks/review provenance and limits. Distinguish fixed/checked content,
verified Git commit/ref/remote, separately verified exact fix-completion and tracker
snapshot/independent read-back. Full `ok` requires every promised effect; admission,
saved snapshot, disposition or Git push alone is insufficient. On a stop name the
cause, preserved effects, uncertainty and exact remaining operation/recovery owner.
Stop at this selected bug's result; never close parents or start another bug.
